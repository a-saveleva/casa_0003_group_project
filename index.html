Features

Typically the travel websites only display selection of places, not overall number of accessible places, not giving the full picture. Also, they don’t typically involve the focus on rail. Our tool addresses this gap in current market and provides advice regarding activities available. We use data from OpenStreetMap (railway network), RSPB and GBIF (for birding density), Natural England (for green space types), and custom-built datasets to generate a composite score for each destination. A “Best Overall” category summarizes general suitability when users haven’t selected a specific activity. By helping people visualize where they can go by train and what they can do there, this project supports broader goals of reducing car dependency while encouraging more people to engage with nearby nature.
